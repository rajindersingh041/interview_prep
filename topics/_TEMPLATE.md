## <Concept name>

**In plain words:** Everyday explanation — short sentences, an analogy if
one fits. No jargon, no formula yet.

**Because:** The mechanism — *why* it works this way. This is the retrieval
cue and the part that makes the fact stick.

**The term:** The technical name and formula, introduced only after the
idea has landed.

**Interview line:** "The exact sentence to say out loud in an interview."

**Trap:** The common mistake to avoid. (optional)

---

## Rules

- Never open a concept with a technical term — the plain-words idea comes first.
- Every formula gets a one-line plain-words reading.
- Include an "Interview framing" section of ready-to-say lines at the end.
- Weak concepts from the diagnostic get a "because" chain and an application
  example, not just a definition.
